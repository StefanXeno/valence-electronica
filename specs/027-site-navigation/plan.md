# Implementation Plan: Site Navigation & Content Overlays

**Branch**: `027-site-navigation` | **Date**: 2026-10-05 | **Spec**: [spec.md](./spec.md)

**Input**: As-built consolidation — documents the shipped implementation; no new work.

## Summary

`SiteNav.astro` renders the top bar, the laptop inline nav, and the phone fullscreen menu
with its own portal copies of Tour / About / Discography / Shop / legal content.
`LegalOverlay.astro` (mounted in `Base.astro`) renders all overlay panels and owns history,
Escape, and deep-link opening. Content routes are generated by `src/pages/[slug].astro`.
